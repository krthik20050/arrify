# STICK IT UP product direction

## Register
Product. The primary surface is a shopping tool: finding a design, checking its price and options, adding it to a cart, and deciding where to place it. The brand supports that workflow.

## Users and purpose
The captured store sells stickers, bumper stickers, specialty finishes, packs and apparel in India. Its product descriptions name laptops, bottles, helmets, journals and other personal objects. A shopper may know a category or may want help discovering designs. The current repository supports real captured catalog data, a local cart, product variants and demo checkout.

The user's stated priority is better usability: a compact single header, less oversized artwork, immediate access to products, clear headings, and less clutter. Discovery should learn from likes/passes on actual sticker designs instead of asking people to classify their personality. Cart stickers should be arrangeable on a laptop layout and optionally over a camera/photo view.

## Brand and anti-references
Keep the existing STICK IT UP logo, playful sticker artwork and black/yellow identity. The products supply colour and personality. Avoid full-screen campaign images, flashing merchandise, overlapping faded headings, repeated discovery labels, fabricated urgency, decorative confetti, gradients and vague AI claims.

## Strategic principles
1. Merchandise and clear navigation before promotion.
2. Every price, product image and availability state comes from the captured catalog.
3. Recommendations are explainable local preference ranking, with undo, reset and access to the full catalog.
4. A manual camera overlay must not be presented as surface-tracked AR or exact physical scale.
5. Preserve the cart and existing shopping contracts during the redesign.
6. Accessible controls are alternatives to gestures. The existing README sets WCAG AA as the target; motion respects reduced-motion settings.

## Evidence
Repository README, captured product descriptions, original homepage/styles, the saved browser screenshots, existing 16-test suite, and the user's 21 October 2026 redesign brief. Real payments/accounts remain owner-integration work.
